# Test checklist — jwan-tech-food-launch-poster

Version tested: 1.0.0

## T1 — Food identity

Input: one named local food.

Pass: texture, serving method, signature feature, and recognizable food identity remain clear.

Fail: the result becomes a generic device or an unrecognizable object.

## T2 — Packaging logic

Pass: cups, sleeves, skewers, leaves, or no packaging match the food brief.

Fail: packaging is decorative but unrelated to the food.

## T3 — Fold structure

Pass: the 16:9 launch-object composition has a readable center hinge or fold logic.

Fail: the foldable-device metaphor is absent or structurally confusing.

## T4 — Launch hierarchy

Pass: the composition stays minimal, central, spacious, and uses short legible copy.

Fail: unrelated logos, watermarks, dense decoration, or excessive text appear.

## T5 — Single finished output

Pass: one coherent poster is returned.

Fail: multiple unrelated designs are combined into one response image.
